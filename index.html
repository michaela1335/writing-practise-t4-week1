The Whispering Lighthouse of Cape Storm

Narrative Prompt:

For decades, the old lighthouse at Cape Storm stood abandoned, its light long extinguished. But during last night's sudden eclipse, the beacon flared back to life with a strange, violet flame—and a low hum began echoing across the cliffs.

Write a narrative story describing your trek up the cliff path, stepping inside the ancient spiral staircase, and what you discover at the top of the tower when you face the origin of the light.

Phase 3 Success Criteria Live Checks

2. Multi-Paragraph Structure (3+ Paragraphs)

3. Sentence Variety & Connectives

Include connectives (although, despite, meanwhile, whereas, nevertheless).

4. Evocative Vocabulary & Imagery

Use vivid sensory terms (violet, hum, spiraling, tempest, roaring, jagged).
